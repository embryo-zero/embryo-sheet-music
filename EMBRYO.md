# Embryo Sheet Music

Embryo Sheet Music is Embryo-Zero's fork of [MuseScore](https://github.com/musescore/MuseScore) (2b9e82d, commit 2b9e82d). It does sheet music writing.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
